06 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is SHBX?

SHBX is the ticker for Shabrix, a Solana-based digital currency.

Is SHBX available to trade?

Yes. SHBX/SOL liquidity is live on Solana. The live market chart above displays current market activity.

What is the total supply?

The total supply is permanently fixed at 1,000,000,000 SHBX. Mint Authority has been revoked.

Can SHBX holder accounts be frozen?

Freeze Authority has been revoked, so the creator cannot freeze SHBX holder accounts through that authority.
